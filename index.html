About me

Hi, I'm Aaron.

A senior game programmer, currently at Ninja Gaming.

I've been making games since 2019. I started as a game designer at BYJU'S, programmed Unity games at PlayShifu, then spent four years as a tools programmer at Kwalee building the editors and pipelines other developers rely on. Since 2025 I've worked as a game programmer at TOJ Games, and now as a senior game programmer at Ninja Gaming.

Outside work I build my own games and tools: a 1v1 multiplayer arena game, a falling-sand simulation, a node-based dialogue editor and a tactical turn-based toolkit for Unity. I also team up for game jams: most recently Pondwise for Comfy Jam: Summer 2026 and To Make This Moment Last for GMTK Game Jam 2026.

I press buttons and the computer does all the work.
